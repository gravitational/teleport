Log in to your cluster before running `tctl`, assigning
<Var name="teleport.example.com:443" /> to your Teleport proxy address and
<Var name="email@example.com" /> to your Teleport username:

```code
$ tsh login --proxy=<Var name="teleport.example.com:443" /> --user=<Var name="email@example.com" />
```
